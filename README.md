# Indian-Tech-Job-Dashboard
Interactive, single-file dashboard analyzing 23,201 Naukri.com tech job listings — role demand, salary bands, top hiring cities, experience-level trends, and skill-domain mix across India's 2026 tech market. Pure HTML/CSS/JS, zero dependencies to run.
1.  Project Overview
The Indian technology job market is highly competitive, making it difficult for job seekers to understand where opportunities are concentrated and what employers are looking for.

This project analyzes 23,201 job listings and presents the findings through an interactive dashboard.

The dashboard answers key questions such as:

Which technology roles have the highest demand?
Which cities have the most job opportunities?
How much salary is offered across different roles?
What experience levels are most commonly required?
What percentage of jobs offer remote or hybrid work?
Which skill domains are most in demand?
How many opportunities are suitable for freshers?
How does company size vary across job listings?

2.  Project Objectives
The main objectives of this project were to:

Analyze India's technology hiring landscape.
Identify high-demand job roles and skill domains.
Compare hiring activity across major Indian cities.
Analyze salary trends across different roles.
Understand experience-level requirements.
Examine remote, hybrid, and on-site opportunities.
Identify fresher-friendly job opportunities.
Present insights through an interactive and user-friendly dashboard.

KPI Metrics

The dashboard provides five key performance indicators:

Average Salary (LPA)
Remote Jobs %
Fresher-Friendly Jobs %

Interactive Visualizations

The dashboard includes:

Jobs by Role Category
Compares hiring demand across different technology roles.
Work Mode Distribution
Shows the percentage of on-site, hybrid, and remote jobs.
Skill Domain Distribution
Analyzes demand across:
Business Intelligence
AI / ML / Deep Learning
Cloud & DevOps
Data Engineering
Data Science
Top Hiring Cities
Identifies cities with the highest number of technology job listings.
Experience Level Demand
Compares demand from fresher-level positions to lead/architect roles.
Average Salary by Role
Compares average salary packages across different job roles.
Company Size Analysis
Shows job distribution across startups, mid-sized companies, and large organizations.
Salary Tier Distribution
Categorizes opportunities into different salary levels ranging from entry-level to leadership positions.

6.  Interactive Filtering

The dashboard supports multiple filters that dynamically update the displayed KPIs and visualizations.

Available Filters
Role
City
Experience Level
Work Mode

Users can combine multiple filters to explore specific segments of the job market.

For example:

Data Analyst + Bangalore + Fresher + Hybrid

This allows users to understand the specific opportunities available within a particular combination of role, location, experience, and work mode.
